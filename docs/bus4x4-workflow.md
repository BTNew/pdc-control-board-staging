## Workshop sequence

1. Arrival and Cars In Sheet, then verified vehicle key.
2. Organise early supplier work while waiting in the yard: tint, signage, flares/hatch covers and seat covers. MMT's first-week expectation is guidance, not a supplier booking.
3. Confirm required parts are available to start and reasonably progress the production stage, then allocate a compatible mechanical bay and technician. Imported parts flags alone do not satisfy this physical readiness check.
4. Mechanical conversion and practical hoist accessories. Running wiring mechanically does not complete final electrical connections.
5. Allow approximately two to three working days before electrical for wheel alignment, rectification and outstanding work. Avoid sending every mechanical build to electrical simultaneously.
6. Electrical, followed by QA/rectification and pit inspection. Plan pit requests 48–72 hours ahead of credible forecast readiness; distinguish a request from a confirmed booking.
7. After pits: rustproofing, wash, existing RFT/QC release process, then customer delivery or transport.

Preserve manual priorities. Changed forecasts flag downstream plans for review; do not silently move bookings, order parts, send supplier requests, or claim work/physical returns complete. Forecasts are planning evidence, not promises. Existing conversion completion and manufacturer Section 12 checks remain mandatory.
